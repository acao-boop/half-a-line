# Art credits: collage room backdrops

Every image in the collages below is public domain or CC0, checked on 19 November 2026 against the source's own statement. The collages themselves (layout, torn edges, tape, cut-paper shapes, paper grain) were made for Half a Line by Claude in `collage-maker.html` and are offered under the same terms. No stock sites (Canva, Unsplash, Getty), no brand names or logos.

- **Met** = The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access: the object record says "Public Domain" and the image is released under **CC0** (https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources).
- **LOC** = Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs: the item's rights statement is quoted.
