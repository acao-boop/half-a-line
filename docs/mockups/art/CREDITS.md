# Art credits: room backdrops

Every photograph and artwork below is public domain or CC0, checked on 19 November 2026 against the source's own statement. The rooms were assembled for Half a Line by Claude in `room-maker.html`: real wall, floor and wood surfaces are cropped from the photographs and repeated; only the picture frames, mats, labels, shadows and light are drawn. No AI image generation, no stock sites (Canva, Unsplash, Getty), no brand names or logos.

- **Met** = The Metropolitan Museum of Art, Open Access: the object record says "Public Domain" and the image is released under **CC0** (https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources).
- **LOC** = Library of Congress, Prints & Photographs: the item's rights statement is quoted. "HABS" = Historic American Buildings Survey (photographs made by the U.S. Government).
