Macbeth

William Shakespeare, c. 1606

Spoken by Macbeth

Life’s but a walking shadow, a poor player / That struts and frets his hour upon the stage / And then is heard no more:

Macbeth has just heard that Lady Macbeth is dead. The image is theatrical on purpose: an actor, on a stage, telling the audience that life is only acting on a stage.
