# Line check, 20 November 2026

Done on branch `claude/lines-151-audit` after Andrew approved days 11–17 and the 91 drafts for days 61–151 (“Reviewed all, approve, do one more audit for word match for all lines”, 20 Nov). This is one more word-for-word check of every line in the game, all 151.

## How it was checked

- **Standard:** the same as `docs/line-check-2026-11-19.md`. Each line must match a named public-domain edition character for character, allowing only a capital on the first word, curly quotation marks, and “ / ” for a line break. Punctuation at the end of an excerpt stays as printed. At least one more independent text must have the same words.
- **Fresh texts:** every Gutenberg, Wikisource and web source named for a line (the 19 Nov report for days 1–60, `docs/drafts/source-check-061-151.json` for days 61–151) was downloaded again on 20 Nov, 468 texts in all, and the line was cut out of each with the comparison in `tools/exact-check.py`. The script that ran it is `tools/audit-all.py`. Scans from archive.org were counted as word-for-word confirmation only, since their punctuation comes from OCR.
- **Result:** 148 lines matched a named edition exactly by script. The other three (26 Heart of Darkness, 31 Huckleberry Finn, 58 To His Coy Mistress) were read by eye: 26 and 31 are the two exceptions accepted on 19 Nov; 58 matches the 1681 text exactly. Every line has at least two independent texts, except where noted below.
- **Pieces:** `node tools/check-lines.js` reports 151 lines, 302 titles, 0 problems: each line's pieces join back into the line, reveal order runs 1 to N, the bonus word is in the line and among its choices, three hints and a note each. Every `theme` matches a painting in `site/weeks.js`, and each theme fills exactly one Sunday-to-Saturday week.
